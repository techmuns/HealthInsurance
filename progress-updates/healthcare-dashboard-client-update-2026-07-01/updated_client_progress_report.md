## The big update — Insights (5 pages)
1. **From static cards to daily market intelligence** — the Insights tab opens on a single guided daily read.
2. **Today's Read** — what changed, why it matters, what to watch next.
3. **Signal Stack** — the fastest signal, a risk watch and an opportunity watch, dated and tagged.
4. **Add to Calendar** — dated signals open pre-filled in Google Calendar (or `.ics` for Apple/Outlook); no sign-in.
5. **Pulse** — correlations, management events and curated signals behind today's story.

## Smarter Data Audit
6. **AI Mode** — select any range of audit cells and ask an AI panel to read them in context.
7. **Instant Visualisation** — the same selection instantly becomes a chart with a quick read and formula.
