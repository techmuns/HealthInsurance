Exclusive - Model and debutante Sana Thampi on being directed by Kiran Rao in Lust Stories 3: ‘She made me feel extremely comfortable and confident’

Fashion model Sana Thampi has taken her first step into acting with Lust Stories 3, starring in one of the anthology’s stories directed by filmmaker Kiran Rao.

Moneycontrol caught with debutante Sana Thampi after the release of her first movie and the model-turned-actress is elated to the positive response her performance as Lisa. Talking about the transition from modelling, Sana said to acting was not something she had planned for years, but the opportunity came her way through an audition and she got the role.

During the exclusive conversation with Moneycontrol, Sana revealed that while giving the audition she was not informed that it was for a Kiran Rao directorial. Sana said she only came to know about it on the first day on the film's set.

Talking about working with Kiran Rao, who has made movies like Laapataa Ladies and Dhobi Ghat, Sana said the filmmaker had a precise understanding of Lisa and made her feel comfortable and confident on set.

Sana further said that she was particularly drawn to her character Lisa’s independence and sense of agency.

Sana said that Kiran, along with writer Tanaji and the rest of the team, made it easier for her to understand Lisa because they already had a very clear idea of who the character was.

“Kiran had an extremely precise idea of who this character is, what she is like, what the situation is like. So it was easy for me to understand who this character was because I had someone who already understood this character,” Sana said.

She added that Kiran made her feel 'extremely comfortable and confident' on set, ensuring that she never felt out of place during her first major acting project.

Journey from modelling to acting

Sana said the opportunity to act came unexpectedly. While models regularly audition for advertisements, she said that opportunities to audition for a lead role in a film or OTT project are relatively rare.

When she received the audition, she was told only that it was for the lead in a short film. After submitting a self-test, she attended her first in-person audition and discovered that the project was actually for Kiran Rao and Lust Stories 3.

Talking about whether there is any similarity between modelling and acting, Sana said the two professions are fundamentally different.

“It’s poles apart. Apart from the fact that I’m in front of the camera, I don’t think there’s any other similarity to it,” she said.

Sana said modelling helped her become comfortable around new teams and unfamiliar people on set, but acting required an entirely different level of preparation and responsiveness.

On playing Lisa in Lust Stories 3

Sana said she did not necessarily see herself in Lisa, but was attracted to the character because she felt like someone she could recognise.

“I liked that she was independent. I liked that she came with a sense of agency,” she said.

When asked if she feared getting stereotyped post Lust Stories 3, Sana said she isn't particularly worried about being typecast because of her modelling background. “I don’t think any of them got stereotyped into glamorous roles. So I don’t see why I would either,” she said.

Since Sana has just stepped into the world of acting, she said she is keeping her options open and wants the opportunity to act rather than entering the industry with a rigid list of roles she wants to play.

Talking about Sana, she was born and raised in Kerala. Sana has spent years building her career as a model and has been associated with some of the biggest names in Indian and international fashion.

She has worked with renonwned fashion designers such as Sabyasachi Mukherjee and Rahul Mishra. She has also featured for Vogue, Supriya Lele, AllSaints, Levi’s and Kay Beauty.

Sana on OTT and South Indian cinema

Sana said she also believes OTT has opened up opportunities for actors, writers and directors who may not always find space in mainstream theatrical cinema.

Sana, who is from Kerala, said she is also keen to work in Malayalam and Tamil films. She said she grew up watching Malayalam cinema and continues to revisit films from the industry.

“In Malayalam films, they do, as an industry overall, give significantly more importance to writers. And I feel like that’s quite an important pillar for the film,” she said.

The response to Sana's performance has so far been positive, and she admits she was nervous about how audiences would react.

For now, she says the response has been encouraging, particularly the comments about her acting and screen presence. And when it comes to social media reactions, Sana has one simple update: “So far, no trolling.”

Talking about Lust Stories 3, Sana was paired opposite Gurfateh Pirzada in Kiran Rao’s story. The anthology brought together four separate narratives around love, desire, intimacy and relationships. It is currently streaming on Netflix.
